About Me

I fell in love with coding at 15, when I wrote my first C++ program. That little success message on the screen hooked me, and I've been building ever since. Over the years, I've developed a strong foundation in problem-solving, logical thinking, and adapting quickly to new stacks.

As an Associate Team Lead, I love digging into the "why" behind every feature, exploring edge cases, and ensuring the final solution is practical, maintainable, and truly useful. I love the frontend for how it brings ideas to life visually, and I value the backend just as much for the control it gives me over data and how it powers those experiences.

Beyond engineering, I've always been drawn to words and ideas. I read close to a hundred books a year, scribble thoughts in my journal, and solve puzzles for fun. I'm also a budding content creator on Instagram, where I share insights on self-care, personal growth, and parenting.
